## Unique Hanzi in New Words

Distinct characters in the numbered New Words entries, listed once in order of first appearance: **教、室、机、场、路、离、公、司、远、共、汽、车、小、时、慢、快、过、走、到** (19 characters).

The warm-up on p. 49 also reviews **考试、商店、机场、路、教室、时间** and prompts learners to name **自行车、公共汽车、出租车、飞机** in pictures. These are activity prompts, not additional numbered New Words entries.

## Characters

The Characters section on printed p. 54 teaches two radicals and gives example characters:

- **彳** (双人旁, “double-person” radical; associated with walking): **行** (xíng, to walk) and **往** (wǎng, to go).
- **攵** (反文旁; traditionally associated with striking/whipping): **放** (fàng, to let go; to set free) and **收** (shōu, to receive; to accept).

These taught characters and examples are separate from the New Words list.
