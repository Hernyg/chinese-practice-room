The p. 113 warm-up matches **新年、票、旅游、帮助、阴、火车站** to pictures; the two unnumbered entries above are warm-up vocabulary rather than numbered New Words. Its picture-naming exercise also prompts learners to name **公共汽车、出租车、火车、自行车** (bus, taxi, train, bicycle); these are picture labels, not entries in the New Words list.

## Proper Names

No proper names are introduced in this chapter. **北京** (Běijīng, Beijing) occurs in the dialogue and is a place name, but is already part of the foundation vocabulary.

## Unique Hanzi in New Words

Distinct characters in the eight numbered New Words entries, listed once in order of first appearance: **日、新、年、票、火、车、站、大、家、更、妹、阴** (12 characters).

## Characters

The Characters section on printed p. 118 introduces two radicals, separately from the New Words list:

- **山** (山字旁): generally associated with names, kinds, and forms of mountains and with islands. Examples: **岭** (lǐng, ridge of a mountain) and **岖** (qū, as in 崎岖, rugged; describing mountain paths).
- **大** (大字旁): generally associated with people. Examples: **天** (tiān, sky) and **夫** (fū, husband).
