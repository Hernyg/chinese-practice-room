## Proper Names

None in the New Words entries. 哥哥 (“elder brother”) and 妈妈 (“mother”) in the lesson are family terms, not proper names.

## Unique Hanzi in New Words

Distinct characters in the numbered New Words entries, listed once in order of first appearance: **课、帮、助、别、哥、鸡、蛋、西、瓜、正、在、手、机、洗** (14 characters).

The p. 73 warm-up reviews or prompts **西瓜、鸡蛋、休息、吃药、手机、准备、午饭** and asks learners to add objects to verbs including **听、踢、玩儿、写、下、开**. These are warm-up words and prompts, not additional numbered New Words.

## Characters

The Characters section on printed p. 78 teaches two radicals and gives example characters:

- **走** (走字旁): associated with running and walking; examples **超** (chāo, to exceed; to surpass) and **起** (qǐ, to get up; to rise).
- **穴** (穴字头): associated with holes, caves, or houses; examples **空** (kōng, empty) and **穿** (chuān, to pierce through; to penetrate).

These radicals and example characters are separate from the New Words list.
