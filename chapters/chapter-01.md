# Chapter 1 — 九月去北京旅游最好

**Pinyin:** Jiǔ yuè qù Běijīng lǚyóu zuì hǎo  
**English title:** September is the best time to visit Beijing  
**Source:** *HSK Standard Course 2* (textbook scan), Chapter 1, printed pages 1–8; source PDF image pages 21–28.
